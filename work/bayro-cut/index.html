Concept study / 05

Bayro Cut

Barbershop · Greve, Denmark

An opening with attitude and a clear hierarchy of services.

Context

A barbershop in Greve, Denmark. The concept is written in Danish.

Proposal

Open with attitude (Dit look. Dit statement.), then lead into a clear hierarchy of services.

Decisions

  1. Black and gold under warm, low light set a cinematic tone.
  2. Condensed headlines carry the attitude; services follow in numbered order: 01 Herreklip, 02 Fade, 03 Skæg.
  3. A gallery of styles helps visitors find their next look.
  4. Ring og bestil tid (call and book) repeats as the main action.
