Concept study / 04

Legend Nails

Manicure · St Albans, UK

An editorial reference, with attention to the finish.

Context

A nail studio in St Albans, UK, offering gel colour, acrylics, manicure, pedicure and nail art.

Proposal

Borrow the pace of a beauty editorial: generous serif type, calm neutrals and close-up photography of the finish.

Decisions

  1. Olive and blush on warm cream, with a lowercase serif wordmark.
  2. A grid of close-ups lets the finish carry the page: shape, colour, detail.
  3. Services are set in small capitals, like an index.
  4. Call to book stays a single, quiet action.
