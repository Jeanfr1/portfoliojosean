Concept study / 01

Automotives STA

Car maintenance · St Albans, UK

The exterior, the parts and the services tell one continuous story.

Context

Car repairs, diagnostics and maintenance for modern performance cars in St Albans, UK.

Proposal

Tell the service through the car itself: the polished exterior first, then the parts beneath it, with every service introduced by the component it takes care of.

Decisions

  1. Black and white with a single red, kept for the key word and the actions.
  2. A wireframe drawn over the bodywork says, from the first screen, that the work happens beneath the surface.
  3. Diagnostics, Repairs and Maintenance are each anchored by a real component: engine, brake disc, filter and tools.
  4. One call to action, Get a quote, opens and closes the page.

Screens

Motion

A scroll sequence in chapters, starting with 01 / The exterior, moves the eye from the body of the car to the parts beneath it.
