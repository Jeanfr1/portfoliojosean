Concept study / 03

Orhan Barber

Barbershop · St Albans, UK

Visual precision, the ritual of the cut and booking always within reach.

Context

A barbershop in St Albans, UK, focused on cuts, fades and beard work.

Proposal

Present the cut as a ritual of precision, and keep booking within reach at every step.

Decisions

  1. Condensed capitals and steel tones echo the edge of the razor.
  2. Close crops of the blade and the fade line show the detail of the craft.
  3. Services stay short and easy to scan: haircuts and fades, beard shaping, texture and perms.
  4. Book your next cut appears in the opening and again at the close, next to the address.

Screens

Motion

A fine line runs from the headline to the razor, tying the promise to the tool.
